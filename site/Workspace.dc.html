What is an annual return, and why three?
An annual return is how much an investment grows (or falls) each year, as a percentage. Real returns vary from year to year and cannot be known in advance, so comparing a lower, a middle and a higher assumption shows how sensitive the outcome is. Negative rates are allowed.

What is inflation?
Inflation is the general rise in prices, usually measured by the Consumer Price Index (CPI). If prices rise faster than your money grows, you can buy less with it. "Real" values remove this effect.
