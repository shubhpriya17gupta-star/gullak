Your data

When you choose "Save my results", Gullak stores in your account:

  • the figures you entered in that tool and the results it showed you,
  • the date you saved them, and which version of the method was used.

They are linked to your account only, not shared or sold, and used only to show them back to you here. Your name and email are kept separately to run your account. Gullak never asks for PAN, Aadhaar, bank or broker details.

Need help, or want us to delete your data for you? Email foundersgullak@gmail.com.

Saved results are a record of what the tool showed on the day you saved it. They are illustrations, not predictions, and not investment advice.
